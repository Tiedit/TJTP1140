CRM = The term can describe strategy, a philosophy, a capability or technological tool to put  
the customer at the center of all activities.
Collect information about the customers and store it in one place.

CRM systems = Technology and people processes to store, share, use and analyze large amounts of customer data.

CRM systems examples:
People: System user - use the technology and carry out processes
Processes: Business processes e.g. sales, customer service, marketing automation
Technology: The CRM system e.g. salesforce and its integrations - an operating platform for people, in which the business processes have been modelled

CRM vendors:
Salesforce, Hubspot, ZOHO, pipedrive, Microsoft Dynamics 365

Traditional CRM triad:
Sales, Marketing and Customer Service = Customer

Front-office tools for the three

Nowadays: CRM core, Customer-facing apps, Platform, Data & AI

CRM implementation project:
A project that aims to bring CRM systems into the customer’s organization 
Often a delivery project ( an external party builds a working software solution for the implementing organization )
Demanding undertaking that touch the implementing organization’s critical processes

Salesforce → Ceili → Lorem

The Iron triangle of ICT projects:
Budget, Schedule, Scope = Project

AI is affecting the scope as people have higher expectations of ICT systems. In reality, however, this is not the case – leading to a disconnect between the customer and vendor’s view of the project.

Project stakeholders:
Customer project team, Customer end users, Customer leaders, Other tech vendors, Our tech vendor, our project team all orbiting around the project

Architect Designs a scalable data model, models the process end to end, makes sure the pieces work together
Developer custom code, apex, lightning web components, data migration and scripting

SAP = ohjelmisto, jolla voidaan hallita yrityksen tai julkisen sektorin alanharjoittajan liiketoimintaprosesseja yhdessä paikassa

SAP-järjestelmällä yleisesti hallitaan seuraavia yrityksen osa-alueita:

Miksi SAP on niin suosittu?
Yksi järjestelmä, yhteinen tieto
Eri osastot tarvitsevat ja käyttävät samoja tietoja
Tietoa ei tarvitse siirtää manuaalisesti järjestelmästä toiseen (CRM → ERP ja toisinpäin)
Prosesseja voidaan automatisoida
Ajantasaista tietoa johdolle päätösten tukemiseksi
SAP tukee suuria käyttäjä- ja tapahtumamääriä

SAP – ei vain yksittäinen ohjelma
